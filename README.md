Biz2Credit is a fintech company founded in 2007 with the mission to provide small businesses with the best funding options through technology that makes business financing easy to understand and easy to access. The main goal is to make business finance work better. Our company leverages technology-driven solutions to help entrepreneurs secure the capital they need to start, manage, and grow their businesses. At Biz2Credit, we aim to simplify the traditionally complex lending process by partnering with small and medium-sized businesses across the United States.
